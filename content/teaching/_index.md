I regularly teach the following courses at FIT:

* NI-RUN **Runtime Systems**
  * Principles of Virtual Machines for graduate students
  * Summer semester, Czech Technical University, since 2024

* NI-APR **Principles of Program Analysis**
  * Introduction to static and dynamic program analysis for graduate students
  * Winter semester, Czech Technical University, since 2020

* BI-OOP **Object-oriented Programming**
  * Advanced concepts of OOP at the end of undergraduate CS program
  * Winter semester, Czech Technical University, since 2018
